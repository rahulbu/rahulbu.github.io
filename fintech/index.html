How they work

Built for revision, not for reading

Search everything

Full-text search across every course in the module, with highlighted snippets. Press / anywhere to jump into the search box, Esc to clear.

Filter to what matters

The ★ High-yield filter narrows the section index to material flagged as near-certain exam content. The Quick-fire filter jumps straight to the condensed revision tables.

One course at a time

A tab strip switches between courses; a sticky section index tracks where you are as you scroll. Nothing else on the page competes for attention.

Print-ready

A dedicated print stylesheet expands every course, keeps table headers repeating across page breaks, and preserves the colour coding. Ctrl/Cmd + P gives you a clean A4 document.

Compiled from the official course transcripts. The cram sheets use their own light theme and won't follow the site's dark mode — that's deliberate, since they're meant to be read for long stretches and printed.
